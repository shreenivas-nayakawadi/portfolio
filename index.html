About

Building for people who have a job to finish

I'm a final-year Information Science student at Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering, working full-time hours on a live ERP that finance and operations teams open every morning. That changes how you write code — a wrong number is not a bug report, it is someone's invoice.

Most of my work sits where the frontend meets real business rules: invoicing, payment lifecycles, reconciliation, dashboards. I like the part where you have to sit with a non-technical user and figure out what they actually need before writing a line.

Outside work I build things end to end — auth systems, commerce platforms, AI tools — because shipping the whole thing teaches you what half-building never does.

Full-stack, genuinely

Schema to pixel. I have owned features from database migration through to the button a user clicks.

Readable over clever
